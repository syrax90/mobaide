Set it up

One install on the computer that runs {{ agent.name }}, one QR scan on your phone. Your computer dials out to the relay, so it works behind any router, NAT or firewall — on mobile data or any Wi-Fi.

  1. 01

    Install the Mobaide agent

    On the computer where {{ agent.name }} is installed and signed in.

    {# One block per command; site.js copies from the , the only place the command lives. #}
    # macOS / Linux 
    curl -fsSL https://get.mobaide.com/install.sh | bash
    # Windows (PowerShell) 
    irm https://get.mobaide.com/install.ps1 | iex
  2. 02

    Scan the pairing QR

    The agent prints a QR code when it finishes installing. Scan it in the app and you are paired — no tokens to copy, nothing to type on the phone. In the browser, sign in at app.mobaide.com instead.

  3. 03

    Start {{ agent.name }}

    Tap New session, pick a workspace, then {{ agent.name }}. It opens as a chat; choose Terminal under Open sessions in for its own interface.

Also in every session

Git, in the session

Read diffs, stage, commit, pull and push from the session's Changes view — run by Git on your computer, with the credentials it already has.

Optional worktrees

Start a session in its own Git worktree, so parallel runs never collide. Opt-in per launch, and Git workspaces only.

Alerts when it needs you

The apps notify you when a chat needs a permission, finishes its turn or fails. A tap opens the session, and the first answer from any of your devices wins.

Several at once

Up to 32 concurrent sessions on one agent, side by side in the session list — {{ agent.name }} next to any other agent or a shell.

Prompt templates

Save a prompt once and insert it at the cursor in any session. Your templates sync across your devices.

Works from anywhere

No public IP, port forwarding, VPN or SSH. Going through the relay, every hop is TLS-encrypted, and the relay never stores your session traffic.

Questions

Is Mobaide the official {{ agent.name }} app?

No. Mobaide is an independent app and is not made by {{ agent.maker }}. It starts the {{ agent.name }} you already have installed and signed in on your own computer, with your settings, and shows it on your phone and in your browser.

Does {{ agent.name }} run on my phone?

No — it runs on your Windows, macOS or Linux computer, in your folders and with your tools. Your phone, tablet or browser is the screen and the keyboard. That is why it can build, test and use Git exactly as it does at your desk.

Is it free?

Yes. Mobaide has no subscription and no paywalled features. {{ agent.name }} uses your own account or API key as it does today: Mobaide does not resell or proxy any model provider.

What leaves my computer?

Session traffic is relayed, not stored: your messages, the agent's replies and any dictation audio pass through the relay's in-memory routing and are then discarded. The relay keeps only what has to sync across your devices — your account, paired devices, prompt templates, saved shortcuts and custom agent types. If you choose Mobaide Cloud for voice input, its speech service keeps the recognised text for up to 14 days to prevent abuse, never the audio. The privacy policy lists every field.
