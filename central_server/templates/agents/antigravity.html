Launch it your way

Pick the model, a sandbox or skip permissions, and additional directories before you start. The sandbox and skip permissions exclude each other, so you cannot pick both by mistake.

Google sign-in from the phone

If agy is signed out, the chat's Sign-in needed card starts Google sign-in on your computer. Paste the code, or the address of the page you land on, and the chat carries on.

No clash with your terminal

The chat holds the same conversation lock as agy. If a conversation is open in another app, Mobaide tells you, and when that app lets go, the chat loads the latest messages before it sends yours.
