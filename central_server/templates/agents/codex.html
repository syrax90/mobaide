Autonomy, your way

Pick Read-only (reads, asks before any change), Auto (edits within the workspace, asks before risky actions) or Full access. Under Advanced, set the sandbox and the approval policy yourself, next to the model and the reasoning effort.

Live with your terminal

When Codex runs in a terminal on your computer, a chat on the phone joins the same session through Codex's shared app-server. A message typed at your desk shows up on the phone, followed by its answer. On macOS and Linux.

Every Codex session

Continue a session lists the Codex sessions stored on your computer, including the ones started in a terminal, and opens one with its history. Delete moves a session to Codex's own archive.

Yes, if you want it to. Auto lets Codex edit within the workspace and asks before risky actions; Full access turns off the sandbox and the approval prompts. You choose in the new-session wizard, and the chat shows the mode in effect on its mode chip.
