How to delete your Mobaide account

You have two ways to permanently remove your Mobaide account and all the data associated with it:

1. From the Mobaide Android app

  1. Open the Mobaide app and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Danger zone.
  4. Tap Delete account, re-enter your password (or re-authenticate with Google), and confirm by typing your email.

2. From this web portal

  1. Sign in to the portal at /portal/login.
  2. Open the Account page.
  3. Scroll to the Danger zone and choose Delete my account.
  4. Re-enter your password and confirm by typing your email.

What gets deleted

  • Your user profile (email, password hash, linked Google account).
  • Every agent you own, and every active session running on those agents.
  • Every device pairing, refresh token, and saved prompt template tied to your account.

{{ grace_days }}-day grace period

When you confirm deletion, your account is immediately signed out everywhere and scheduled for permanent removal in {{ grace_days }} days. Within that window you can restore your account by signing back in (either in the app or this portal) and choosing Cancel scheduled deletion, or by clicking the cancel link in the confirmation email.

After {{ grace_days }} days, the deletion is final and cannot be reversed.

Audit log

We retain a minimal deletion-audit record (timestamp, source, and the email as it was at request time) so we can answer compliance questions about deleted accounts. This record never contains your password, agents, or session data.

Need help? Reach out from a signed-in session in the portal or the app and we'll walk you through it.
